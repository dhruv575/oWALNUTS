- Conclusion: not supported — `MomentumSum` is not a uniform improvement on the posteriordb set (a per-model coin, 0.78–2.14x, 1.06x geomean inside seed noise, one cell fewer) and is not made the default; `Tuning::default()` keeps `UTurnRule::Endpoints`, `KernelOptions` stays opt-in, no kernel or test change. Supported: the rule is safe (funnel tail mass at the paper tuning, Eight Schools 1.08x) and the right opt-in for kidiq/garch/nes-like targets. Also found: the per-gradient gap to CmdStan on healthy models is not the U-turn rule (0.434x -> 0.462x), and the sampler-default funnel is biased under every U-turn rule (z −3 to −11 over three seeds), a `Tuning::default()`-on-funnels finding that `freeze_mode_v1`'s one seed did not resolve.
- Next decision: (1) the dual-averaging step collapse on `sblrc` / `earnings` / `diamonds` (`h ~ 0.003`, hundreds of depth-10 caps, where the stan-style preset gets 2–10x per gradient) is now the largest per-gradient item; (2) the sampler-default funnel bias (four levels, `h0 = 0.5`) needs its own study before any funnel claim at the defaults — the documented funnel configuration remains the paper tuning; (3) per-model heterogeneity of the U-turn rule (gains where orbits lengthen on the 66-d GP and the HMM, losses on the depth-capped regressions) suggests the rule choice is a target property, not a default.
