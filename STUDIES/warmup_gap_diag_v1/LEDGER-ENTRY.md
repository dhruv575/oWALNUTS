Statistic: min bulk ESS per target call (warmup included), seed medians, ratio to the shipped defaults; ArviZ 0.23.4.
- Diagnosis: on the 14 CmdStan-healthy models warmup gradients are 1.56x CmdStan's while sampling gradients per effective sample are about 1.05x; pooled per window the initial fast phase is 2.07x (24 % of the excess), the slow mass windows 1.14–1.27x (5–8 % more leaves per transition, 8 % smaller step), the terminal window 1.01x. Two mechanisms: (1) a single-leaf orbit ending in a reverse-coarser rejection feeds dual averaging a `CurrentCoarseEndpoint` statistic of 0.02–0.36, cutting the step 3–10x in one iteration, followed by 5–10 depth-10 recovery orbits; 21–46 such crashes per chain on every model; (2) from `h0 = 0.5` the first dual-averaging iterates climb to `h` of 1–6 because fully refined leaves still report an acceptable statistic, the first 3–4 transitions cost about 1,146 gradients each, then the step overshoots to about 0.006.

- Conclusion: every arm that recovers warmup gradients ends at a larger adapted step and loses on the four target models; the warmup gap and the target-model robustness lead are the same behaviour (the shipped statistic cuts the step hard on one failed refined leaf). The bar (>= 1.10x with no model < 0.90x) is not met by any arm; nothing is preregistered.
