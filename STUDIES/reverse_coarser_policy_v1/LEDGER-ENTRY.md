- Diagnostics: prediction P1 (adapted `h` within 5 %) **falsified** — `h` rose on 16 of 17 models, 3–30 % (geomean 1.13x). Mechanism: the shipped `CurrentCoarseEndpoint` statistic averages `exp(-|dH|)` over every built leaf and a zero-weight leaf is a built leaf, so continuing past a failed leaf dilutes its low value and dual averaging installs a larger step; the larger step raises the refined fraction 2–25x and the failure count 1.3–26x, and `beyond` carries those failures as zero-weight leaves (4–19 % of built leaves on most models, 57 % on `accel_gp`, whose leaves per orbit went 321 → 507 and mean depth 7.6 → 9.0). The study therefore compared "larger step with zero-weight tails" against "shipped step with truncation", not truncation alone. `artifacts/results-table.md`, `artifacts/summary.json`, `artifacts/cells/`, `artifacts/funnel/`, run logs.
