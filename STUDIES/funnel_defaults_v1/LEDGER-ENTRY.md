### WP28-FUNNEL-DEFAULTS-V1 — the sampler defaults halve the funnel's tail mass; eight refinement levels fix it at no measured cost and become the default
- Ordered time: 2026-09-02 on `wt/funnel-defaults`; preregistration, protocol and driver committed (`ef62362`) before the first evidence cell; 81 cells in one strictly sequential run of about two minutes; summary immediately after; default applied in the labelled final commit.
- Protocol/config: `STUDIES/funnel_defaults_v1/{PREREGISTRATION.md, protocol.json}` (hashes in the study `CHECKSUMS.sha256`). 10-D Neal funnel (paper definition, starts `omega in {-3, -1, 1, 3}`), 4 chains x 2,000 / 20,000, `Sampler` at its 0.2.0 defaults (adapted diagonal, dual averaging 0.8 with the WP24 warmup rule, `h0 = 0.5`, depth 10, four levels, `delta = 1`, cache, worst-case admission) plus one override per arm: `defaults`, `levels8`, `delta0.5`, `delta0.25`, `levels8+delta0.5`, `paper-4`, `paper-8`, `stan-style`, `nuts-1` (one level, control). Cost cells on the noncentered Eight Schools (strict-track density and starts) and a 100-D Gaussian (`Init::uniform`), 4 x 1,000 / 1,000, same arms. Tail-mass `z` from the ArviZ MCSE of the indicator; cost = mean bulk ESS per retained call, seed median, vs `defaults`. Decision rule: cheapest arm with |z| <= 2 on all three seeds and >= 0.9x on both cost targets; else cheapest unbiased; else README note + `Tuning::funnel()`.
- Seeds: 82101–82103 (verified unused). Deviations: none; the `stan-style` 82103 funnel cell errored (nonfinite target value on the non-recoverable path after the preset's `delta = 1000` fast phase) and is reported as a failure of the arm, not rerun.
- Status: preregistered grid; default changed in the final commit.
- Outcome: `defaults` biased low on two seeds (0.0203 z −3.5, 0.0242 z −3.75, 0.0625 z +0.3; 2,933 retained exhaustions, 54 divergences over three seeds) — P1 held. `levels8` within |z| <= 2 on every seed (0.0412 −0.34, 0.0346 −1.43, 0.0897 +1.00; 113 exhaustions, 8 divergences) at 1.08x the funnel calls, **1.05x** Eight Schools ESS/call, **1.00x** Gaussian (two seeds call-for-call identical) — P3, P6 held. `levels8+delta0.5` also unbiased (−1.88, −0.80, −0.68), zero divergences, better `omega` R-hat (<= 1.017 vs 1.011–1.037) at 0.74x funnel calls but 0.79x on the Gaussian. `delta0.5` / `delta0.25` alone worsen the bias (11,000–12,000 exhaustions; two seeds short each). `paper-4` biased; `paper-8` from `h0 = 0.5` missed one seed (0.0293, z −2.72) and paper mode is 0.39–0.41x on the Gaussian. `stan-style` biased with 2,132 divergences on one seed and an errored cell. `nuts-1` shows the NUTS failure (two seeds with no draw below −5; 78,469 exhaustions) — P2 held. Caveat: at eight levels one chain per seed still adapts to `h` = 0.007–0.019 and either never leaves or never enters the neck; the pooled estimate is right, the `omega` R-hat is 1.01–1.04 and its bulk ESS ~100 per 80,000 draws — the default is unbiased on the funnel, not efficient on it.
- Diagnostics: `artifacts/results-table.md`, `artifacts/summary.json`, `artifacts/cells/*.json` (per-chain tail mass, final `h` and `delta`, ESS on every coordinate, health counts), `artifacts/run-log.txt`.
- Artifacts: `STUDIES/funnel_defaults_v1/{PREREGISTRATION.md, protocol.json, README.md, LEDGER-ENTRY.md, Cargo.toml, Cargo.lock, src/main.rs, run_grid.sh, CHECKSUMS.sha256, artifacts/}`.
- Conclusion: supported — (a) the 0.2.0 sampler defaults are biased on Neal's funnel (half the tail mass) because four halvings of the adapted step cannot enter the neck; (b) `max_refinement_levels = 8` removes the bias on every seed and is free on the ordinary targets (the cap never engages there), so it is the new `Tuning::default()`. Not supported: a lower `delta` as a fix (it makes the four-level bias worse); `Adaptation::Paper` from the sampler's `h0 = 0.5` as an unbiased default (one seed missed, and 0.4x on the Gaussian); the `stan_style` preset on this target.
- Next decision: (1) the per-chain step collapse on the funnel under dual averaging (one chain per seed at `h ~ 0.01`, R-hat 1.01–1.04 at eight levels) is the remaining funnel defect at the defaults — the same mode as the `sblrc`/`earnings` collapse in WP25 and the `arma11` post-escape crawl in WP24, so one adaptation study should address all three; (2) `levels8+delta0.5` is the funnel-efficient setting and should be re-measured on posteriordb before it is offered as more than a README note; (3) the hand-written funnel example should map every nonfinite density to the recoverable path, as BridgeStan does.
