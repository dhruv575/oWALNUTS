- Ordered time: 2026-10-24 on `wt/metric-regularisation` (from `main` `3583d130`). - Change (opt-in, defaults and kernel fingerprints unchanged; `cargo test` debug and release green): `DiagonalMetricRegularization::ScaleFreeMedian` installs `(n/(n+5)) var_i + (5/(n+5)) m`, where `m` is the median of the window variances. `ScaleFreeStanMedian` installs `(n/(n+5)) var_i + 1e-3 (5/(n+5)) m`.
