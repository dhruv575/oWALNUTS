- Conclusion: supported — (a) the integration layer is fixed: no oWALNUTS cell dies on NaN/inf or an unevaluable start, and per-gradient wall is below CmdStan's; (b) depth 10 and the v4 paper rule remove the v1 regression failures; the paper rule is safe but not better than DA; (c) the breadth gap is now entirely kernel-side: 0.4–0.9x CmdStan per gradient on healthy models plus two models where chains freeze from starts where every leaf fails (`arma11`, `lotka_volterra`), which NUTS's reject-and-shrink survives. Not supported: the breadth-throughput claim (the release gate P1–P3+P5 is not met: P1 by one cell, P2 by a factor of two); any claim that refinement helps here.
- Next decision: (1) the frozen-chain mode — a start where every leaf is a recoverable failure at every refinement level and `h -> 0` — needs a kernel rule (treat an all-exhausted transition as a NUTS reject with a step shrink, or fall back to `delta = divergence threshold` until the first completed orbit, as the stan-style ramp partly does) and a targeted study on `arma11`/`lotka_volterra` starts; (2) the per-gradient gap on healthy models (U-turn/selection rules, reverse-check overhead) is the remaining efficiency target; (3) v3 of this protocol after (1) is the release gate for a breadth claim.
