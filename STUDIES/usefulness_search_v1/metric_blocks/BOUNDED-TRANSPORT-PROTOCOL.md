# Bounded quadratic-feature mechanism screen

Freeze K=4 before fitting. Replace only quadratic features e_i*e_k by phi(e_i)*phi(e_k), where phi(t)=K*tanh(t/K); retain ordinary linear features. Refit the same400 training positions per fold with the unchanged affine covariance regularization, ridge10 and residual-scale checks. All four folds and their separate8000-point evaluation samples remain. No new posterior draws: this is an exposed mechanism screen, not validation.

The triangular map e_j=s_j*z_j+b_j+f_j(e_<j), s_j>0, remains globally invertible recursively, with constant determinant det(L)*product(s_j). Since |phi|<=K and |phi'|<=1, each derivative |df_j/de_i| is bounded by |linear_ji| plus K times the absolute incident quadratic coefficients (twice for the diagonal term). Let B contain those strict-lower bounds and S=diag(s). Then entrywise |de/dz|<=(I-B)^(-1)S, a finite sum because B is nilpotent. Hence |dq/dz|<=|L|(I-B)^(-1)S globally. This guarantees finite derivative bounds for finite coefficients, not good conditioning or easy posterior sampling. The retained linear terms need not be bounded, and no uniqueness/novelty claim is made.

Check encode/decode roundtrips at all evaluation positions (relative1e-9), finite transformed scores, decoder Jacobian/constant determinant and Gaussian-target chain-rule gradients at three points/fold (scaled1e-6). Compute and save the global derivative bound, but do not equate it with practical stiffness.

Bulk advancement requires every fold: bounded/affine Gaussian score discrepancy<.8, bounded/unbounded discrepancy<=1.25, and bounded-minus-unbounded Gaussian cross-entropy<=.05 nat/dimension. Compare map Jacobians at the SAME physical endpoints of all76 recorded divergent proposals, after re-encoding into each map; do not compare unequal physical points at the same z. Require median pointwise ratio of largest singular values<.5 and95th percentile<=.75. These diagnostic thresholds cannot establish chain mixing or cause of original divergences. Preserve failures, original polynomial maps and the failed validation. If this screen passes, compiled-target equivalence and fresh equally controlled native/WAL experiments are still required.
