# Statistical gains do not yet establish practical usefulness

All 96 workers completed, covering 192 independent stationary-start replicates paired between ordinary and conditional estimators. Exact draws, target calls and emitted work counters matched. Independent replay verified all 192 paired estimate vectors, 88 individual functional MSE ratios and 12 target/family ratios. Three independent quadrature calculations agreed for the centered funnel's non-omega marginal tail probability. No aggregate mean-error z flag exceeded 4.5. This screen does not establish cold-start convergence or asymptotic variance reduction.

Statistical MSE ratios (ordinary/conditional) were 1.23-1.70 for the Gaussian families, apart from rotated second moments at 1.33 (within that range); the funnel ratios were only 1.02-1.04. All functionals, uncertainty and split-half sensitivity remain in RECYCLING-MSE-ANALYSIS.json. Heavy-tailed funnel second moments must not be treated as well-characterized just because their paired ratio is close to one.

The original supervisor-based timing suggested near-zero overhead and is preserved but not accepted as a runtime conclusion. Its .1-second polling interval overwhelmed these short jobs. A separately frozen randomized direct timing replay reproduced the canonical outputs in all 288 executions. It measured total cost ratios of 1.360 [1.314,1.400] for rotated Gaussian, 1.726 [1.697,1.756] for noncentered Gaussian, and 1.498 [1.468,1.534] for the funnel. These include process execution, serialization and file close, not durable fsync. Sampler-internal overhead was roughly 19-21%; output sizes grew about 3.8-4.1 times.

After direct cost adjustment, only rotated-Gaussian P(x<-2) retained a descriptive range wholly above one: ratio 1.246, conservative endpoint range [1.053,1.491]. This range is not a calibrated joint confidence interval. Means and second moments on that target were around parity. The ten-dimensional Gaussian and funnel families did not establish a total-cost gain; the funnel was around .68-.70. This rejects a general improvement claim for the current full-recording prototype. The narrow Gaussian-tail lead is exposed and analytic, not an actual application or uniqueness result. Comparably recycled native NUTS remains untested.

## Next reviewed implementation question

The current observer serializes every per-transition observable, even when the requested result is a mean or tail probability. Test a compact estimator-output mode that computes the same conditional summaries and retains iteration/batch information sufficient for honest uncertainty, instead of returning all intermediate vectors. Compare against an equally compact ordinary-output mode, preserve exact underlying chain trajectories and counts, and explicitly account for allocations, aggregation and serialization. First verify numerical equivalence against these preserved full records, then freeze fresh replicate/timing validation. Do not disguise storage reduction as new statistical information or treat batch summaries as independent draws.

If the compact version retains a substantive useful gain, proceed to qualified expensive posterior models and comparably recycled strong NUTS, with a prespecified application-facing uncertainty functional. Otherwise close the proposal. No production change, deployment, trading or goal completion is justified by this screen.
