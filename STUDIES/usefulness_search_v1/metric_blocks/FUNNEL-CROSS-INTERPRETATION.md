# Retained threshold, not just warmup, causes the observed tail failure

All 16 crossed fits completed and all independent replays passed. With common starts, fixed per-chain settings and fresh seeds, all eight max_error=1000 continuations recorded zero visits to omega below -5, whose exact probability is 0.04779035. This occurred with both default-trained and permissive-trained settings. At max_error=1, all eight continuations visited that region; estimates ranged from 0.0297125 to 0.0622875. Their MCSE remained too large, and every cell had depth caps when including the fixed-settings discard. None qualified. All 16 had zero divergences including discard.

The earlier failure cannot be attributed solely to permissive warmup choosing bad parameters. Switching retained threshold changes the result while holding settings, starts and seeds fixed. This is evidence about finite-run exploration, not proof of stationary bias or a kernel correctness defect. Better-looking estimates at threshold 1 do not meet the full accuracy guard and do not establish an improvement.

Preflight passed finite-difference/change-of-variables checks, exact draws and call parity against the earlier fixed-metric Gaussian runner, two independent-Gaussian controls, two exact single-chain replays and rejection of a negative mass. The exported settings matched warmup-end settings in all 32 pilot chains. The analysis reuses the frozen statistical functions; an independent implementation replayed all 320 moment estimates, gates, common starts, counts and source hashes. Recorded telemetry conservatively includes the 2,000 fixed-settings discarded transitions. Prior pilots are exposed and their full cost is separate; no cold-workflow speed claim is made.

## Close this tuning branch

Do not retune permissive error from these failures. Prior research also rejected warmup-error-quantile rules (`STUDIES/adaptive_delta_v1/README.md`), general longer-warmup/initial-step-search intervention (`STUDIES/neal_funnel_intervention_pilot_v2/README.md`) and simple telemetry warnings (`STUDIES/silent_funnel_diagnostic_v1/README.md`). Repeating these ideas without a distinct mechanism is not useful progress.

## Next research queue: observable reuse with correct selection weights

Audit whether already-computed trajectory states can improve estimates of posterior expectations and tail probabilities without changing the chain. This is a different objective from reducing calls or treating more stored points as independent draws. Recycling HMC/NUTS is established work: Nishimura and Dunson, *Recycling Intermediate Steps to Improve Hamiltonian Monte Carlo*, Bayesian Analysis (2020), [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.06925v3). Their NUTS construction uses valid selection sets and per-iteration normalization; arbitrary intermediate points are not automatically posterior draws. No novelty claim is warranted from recycling itself.

The source audit must start with `src/kernel.rs::combine_metropolis_observed`, `combine_barker_observed` and subtree stopping rules. The shipped outer rule is biased progressive selection, with update probability min(1, exp(new.log_weight-old.log_weight)); it is not the normalized mixture used by the alternative. Derive the conditional observable expectation through the actual selection recursion, including rejected/invalid subtrees and the initial state. Do not use a simple uniform average of all integrator points or count each recycled point as an independent iteration.

Only after a mathematical and code-path audit: build an observation-only research implementation, prove tiny-tree weight identities, preserve bit-exact draws/random consumption/target calls, and verify exact moments on analytic targets. Then freeze replicated MSE-per-total-cost comparisons on means, second moments and tail indicators, with iteration-level uncertainty and a comparably recycled NUTS control. Gains are not guaranteed, particularly for antithetic means. Reject the branch if the conditional selection law cannot be justified or if gains are merely extra storage/incorrect ESS accounting. This queue is a research proposal, not a verified improvement or completed application.
