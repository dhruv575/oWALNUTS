# End-to-end comparison requirements after qualified confirmation

Status: design notes, not an executable or frozen experiment. Both sampler and conditional-importance accuracy confirmations are now qualified; gradient-count comparisons do not answer the runtime question.

An honest transformed-sampler workflow must rerun the complete original pilot (four chains,1100 warmup,2000 retained,native low-rank/.99), extract the prescribed first100 retained positions per chain, fit the fixed K=4 map, serialize its target data, then run production and diagnostics. The400 training positions are not the entire pilot cost. Replay existing pilot seeds first to verify identical draws/counts/maps before trusting a timing adapter. Such replay establishes reproducibility and timing of a fixed workflow, not new-map generalization. New pilots may fail and must not be silently retried until a desirable map appears.

For conditional importance, rerun grid preparation and all preflight work, then the unchanged production proposal/weighting and24-quantity diagnostics. Include conditional optimization and backend factor extraction, not just exact densities. Shared compilation can be excluded only as an explicit common precompiled-service scenario; report cold process imports separately from in-process inference cost. Never combine differently scoped historical clocks into a purported end-to-end measurement.

Use serial jobs and alternating or randomized workflow order, with recorded machine/resource state. Compare matched24-function accuracy first; effective samples from importance and MCMC are not directly interchangeable. Precision can be described using the worst standardized mean MCSE and reference discrepancies, with each method's qualified uncertainty calculation and fresh replicate errors. A final protocol must specify budgets and comparison gates before collecting timings; fixed-budget runtime alone does not imply precision-matched superiority.

Retain raw and affine native controls, bounded native on the same fitted maps, raw WALNUTS and bounded WALNUTS, and the successful conditional baseline. Preserve setup costs symmetrically. Any practical or substantive algorithm-improvement claim still needs evidence beyond this exposed synthetic problem, and the submission-ready paper needs a defensible contribution and related-work positioning. The current transport construction is established methodology, not itself proof of novelty.
