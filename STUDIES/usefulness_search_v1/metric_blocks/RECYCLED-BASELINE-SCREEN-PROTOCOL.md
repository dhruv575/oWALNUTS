# Independent recycled baseline elimination screen

Frozen 2026-10-30, before fresh sampling. Primary: normalized second-moment family MSE on the 10-dimensional noncentered Gaussian (variances 9,1,...,1). Secondary: means and probabilities below 0/-2; rotated Gaussian covariance [[2,1],[1,2]] across all four families. These are analytic screening targets, not an application or novelty claim.

64 independent stationary initial points per target, PCG64 seeds 941600001+target index (noncentered first, rotated second). Four chains per batch, sampler seeds 941610001+1000*target index+replicate. WALNUTS ordinary and recycled: 1,000 retained transitions, no warmup, step .3, max_error 1, eight levels, identity diagonal for noncentered and exact inverse covariance for rotated. These exposed settings are frozen; do not optimize on new results. Native NUTS ordinary and recycled, diagonal and low-rank: same starts and numeric seeds (different RNG/trajectories), 1,000 warmup plus 1,000 retained transitions, maxdepth 10, no jitter. All binaries Rust 1.99.1. Source-native controls, not installed-wheel equivalence.

This deliberately optimistic WAL screen does not charge its metric selection/adaptation, while charging native adaptation. It can eliminate a candidate that fails even this advantage; it cannot establish a fair cold-start advantage. A pass requires a separate matched cold-start experiment, qualified nonanalytic posterior and application-relevant evidence. Previous within-WAL improvements are not comparative superiority.

Six arms, 16 batches per target, three process-timing repetitions in randomized order (PCG64 seed 941630001+100*target index+batch). Direct perf_counter around subprocess completion, including summary generation/output but not fsync. Timing repetitions must reproduce canonical outputs excluding only seconds. Statistical replicates remain 64, not 192. Compare underlying hashes and work for ordinary/recycled pairs. Native ordinary summaries and recycled summaries both computed in observed arm; WAL observed computes recycled summaries only, another optimistic cost asymmetry reported explicitly.

For each family normalize coordinate errors by sqrt(variance) for means, variance for second moments, and 1 for probabilities. Compare native MSE / WAL recycled MSE and native total process time / WAL recycled total process time. Their product is the descriptive efficiency ratio favoring WAL above 1 (MSE times cost criterion). Bootstrap whole paired replicate vectors (10,000 resamples, PCG64 941620001), and whole timing batches (10,000, PCG64 941630101). Product endpoint ranges are descriptive conservative ranges, not calibrated joint intervals. Report all six arms against exact truths and all ratios. Primary advances only if cost-adjusted lower endpoint exceeds 1.2 against BOTH recycled native variants, and no coordinate aggregate mean-error |z|>4.5. An ordinary native control that wins also vetoes promotion. No added seeds/gate changes. Equality of numeric seeds does not imply common random numbers.

Native qualification from previous full runs is necessary but not sufficient for this screen. Report divergences, per-coordinate aggregate errors, and nominal block-MCSE coverage on fresh runs for every arm; block coverage is diagnostic, not certified uncertainty. Preserve failed gates and any unexpected mismatch before further studies. Sequential 180-second/2-GiB/64-MiB batch workers, 1-GiB disk floor. No paid cloud, deployment or trading.
