# Curved recycling qualification

Frozen 2026-10-30 before execution. Audit: curvature_scaling_v1 already tested dimensions 8/24, curvature .5/2/5 with fresh held-out tuning, native baselines and exact Gaussian reparameterization. The strongest (24,5) target had an approximately 1.97x target-call lead but no wall-time lead, and exact reparameterization was about ten times cheaper. Do not repeat that study as a new finding. Recycling is the new mechanism under test, not curvature tuning itself.

Implement only the existing all-pairs 24D curvature-5 target: independent standard-normal z, x[2j]=z[2j], x[2j+1]=z[2j+1]+5*(z[2j]^2-1). Direct-coordinate density has unit Jacobian; latent-coordinate density is standard normal. Shared analytic target code in all four drivers, and original-coordinate observables in both direct and latent representations. Both kernel snapshots remain isolated; only observable maps change. The original production kernel is unchanged.

Qualify analytic gradients and map/inverse identities, including finite overflow behavior. Verify original/disabled/enabled draw hashes and actual work for direct and latent representations, plus compact/full-output replay. Native diagonal and low-rank adaptation both covered, with 1,000 warmup and 4,000 retained draws. WAL fixed kernels use no warmup, 4,000 draws, diagonal inverse marginal variance (1,1/51) and step .1 for direct, identity and step .3 for latent, eight levels/error1. This is qualification only, no timing comparison or tuning selection.

Four stationary starts per representation: PCG64 941700001 in latent space, transformed for direct. Sampler seeds 941710001 + 100*representation_index + chain_index. Exact original means zero, second moments (1,51), and probabilities from independently checked Gaussian-mixture quadrature. Require latent known-truth mean error <=.05 marginal SD, second-moment error <=10%, probability error <=.02; raw latent Rhat<=1.01, bulk/tail ESS>=400, zero retained divergences. Heavy-tailed original squares motivate the prespecified 10% qualification tolerance, not a precision claim. Direct runs are parity/diagnostic evidence only until independently qualified; failures remain visible.

Four native/WAL pristine/observed packages, one compiler Rust 1.99.1; original source/dependency identities preserved. Builds 300s/4GiB; sampling 180s/2GiB/64MiB, serial BLAS1, free disk >=1GiB. Do not promote a synthetic result as a use case. A later efficacy study needs independent seeds, properly charged adaptation, strong controls, and exact reparameterization/direct-independent-draw comparisons.
