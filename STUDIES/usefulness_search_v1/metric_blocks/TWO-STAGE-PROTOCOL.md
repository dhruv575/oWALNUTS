# Equal-work two-stage integrator feasibility screen

Frozen2026-10-30 before execution. Previous delta controllers, permissive thresholds, reverse-check alterations and pooling do not provide qualified practical gains. Search of repository Markdown/Rust found no Omelyan/BCSS or two-/multi-stage integrator experiment. Test a different numerical map without changing any production code. Prior art: Blanes, Casas and Sanz-Serna, Numerical Integrators for the Hybrid Monte Carlo Method, DOI10.1137/130932740, https://www.gicas.uji.es/Fernando/MyPapers/93274-gg.pdf . Published symmetric splitting methods are not novel here.

Maps: ordinary kick-drift-kick leapfrog; two-stage kick(b*h), drift(h/2), kick((1-2b)*h), drift(h/2), kick(b*h). Candidate coefficients b=(3-sqrt(3))/6 and b=0.1931833275037836. Cached initial gradient; two fresh gradients per complete two-stage step. Compare eight leapfrog steps of sizeh against four two-stage steps of size2h: same integration time and eight new fused target evaluations. b=.25 must agree numerically with two leapfrog half-steps. No acceptance or cost benefit follows merely from smaller local error.

Independent exact stationary positions and standard Gaussian momentum, identity mass. Targets:24D Gaussian;24D paired banana with curvature2 and5;10D funnel omega~N(0,9), x|omega~N(0,exp(omega)). Fixed seeds944100001+target index,8192 phase points each. Gaussian h=[.25,.5,1,1.5]; all other h=[.01,.03,.1,.3]. No tuning or stopping based on results. Common phase points per target, all maps/steps. Physical-coordinate jump squared divided by exact marginal variance, averaged over coordinates, weighted by min(1,exp(-deltaH)); nonfinite paths count as rejection/zero jump. Also report mean acceptance, finite fraction, energy-error quantiles. Every failed path retained. For funnel variance of x is exp(4.5); means0. Banana variance alternates1 and1+2*c*c.

Checks before screening: analytic gradients by finite differences on non-extreme points, symmetric forward/backward map, finite-difference Jacobian determinant near1 on2D Gaussian and banana; b=.25 equivalence. Require reversibility error<1e-8, Jacobian determinant error<1e-5, gradient error<1e-5. Numerical checks supplement, not replace, symmetric splitting argument.

Paired8192 phase points are experimental units;2000 bootstrap resamples, seed944200001+100*target_index+10*step_index+candidate_index. Ratios of mean accepted normalized squared jump (same call budget) and95% percentile intervals. Exploratory family of32 comparisons, no multiplicity-adjusted or application claim. Advance a candidate only if at least one curved-target step with baseline acceptance in[.5,.95] has lower ratio bound>1.05, and no qualifying curved step has upper bound<.85. Gaussian-only wins are insufficient. If promoted, next work is isolated Rust integration with accounting, symmetry/refinement correctness and full cold/adapted comparisons against original WALNUTS, native NUTS and reparameterized controls on fresh seeds.
