# Bounded-prior confirmation design preparation

Status: pre-acquisition draft, not an admitted sampling protocol. No candidate
prices, returns or scores have been read in preparing this document.

Candidate panels are September and October 2025, the latest two complete months
before the November context used in the exposed December-February study. Calendar
order and project access history determine this candidate, not market outcomes.
The refreshed metadata inventory found no overlapping request interval from
August 26 through October 31, 2025. It inspected 1,897 metadata files and six ZIP
members, with zero parse errors or oversized metadata skips. This bounded local
audit excludes unexpanded tar archives, remote-only metadata and external access;
it cannot yet establish complete project non-exposure. Audit those remaining
research inventories before admission. Do not call this a prospective holdout.

The intended primary evaluation uses every half-hour origin in both months,
not sparse sentinel-only score comparisons: 1,440 September and 1,488 October
origins, with causal 250-return windows and next-observation labels. Preserve
all repeated values and failed slots. Keep cap 1 as the fixed central prior
specification, not a development-score winner; caps 0.5 and 2 remain declared
sensitivity targets on a calendar-fixed subset to be specified before acquisition.
Unchanged rolling t5, EWMA t5 and NIG AR1 are required controls.

Compare qualified NUTS and WALNUTS at the existing fixed budgets before any claim
about inference substitution. Separate model-versus-baseline score differences
from WALNUTS-versus-NUTS differences. The central question is persistence of gains
against EWMA and rolling t5 across both months, not selection of whichever
baseline or month looks favorable. Keep all-origin means primary; influential
origins, medians, tails and PIT are disclosed secondary diagnostics.

Before data acquisition, freeze exact indexing, sampler seeds and their global
collision audit, effect/precision requirements, serial-dependence uncertainty
procedure, multiplicity handling, failure policy, diagnostic gates, resource
budgets and archive schedule. Existing short-batch runtime suggests a sustained
queue is feasible, but storage and job supervision must support the whole
denominator without skipping cases. No optional stopping on score significance.

Predictive confirmation does not resolve the continuous observation approximation,
prove unique sampler benefits, or establish AMM decision value. Mathematical
review, fee/execution identification, inventory constraints and matched economic
controls remain separate requirements of the full objective.
