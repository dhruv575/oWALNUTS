### WP47-PYTHON-CALLPATH-V1 — per-call cost and ESS/s of every Python entry point against nutpie; two bit-identical fixes (thread-state pinning for callables at threads > 1, `from_jax` without `jnp.asarray`) pass the merge rule

- Ordered time: 2026-10-24 on `wt/python-callpath` (base `3583d130`). Preregistration, protocol and harness were committed at `91305996` before any evidence cell. Amendments: A1 (`4f9eee9c`, after the smoke test and before phase 1); A2 (`5d30b70a`, after the first hier cfunc errors); A3 plus the phase-2 addendum (`60f745d2`, after phase 1); A4 (`d9c83258`, fix implementation); A5 (`b9c94051`, after phase 1b). Each was committed before the cells it governs. Phases ran strictly sequentially: phase 1, then 1b, then 2, then 1c. Fix commit: `06a551a7`.
- Protocol/config: `STUDIES/python_callpath_v1/{PREREGISTRATION.md, protocol.json}`. Targets: 100-D standard Gaussian, noncentered Eight Schools, and a 157-D noncentered hierarchical regression (50 groups x 20 observations, data seed 4747900), written in numpy, numba, JAX, PyTorch and PyMC on a shared unconstrained layout. Arms: plain numpy callable, plain callable around a numba njit kernel, numba cfunc of the same kernel (later also guarded and `error_model="numpy"` variants), `from_jax`, `from_torch`, `from_pymc` (C backend), `from_pymc(mode="NUMBA")`, `from_pymc(gil_free=True)` and nutpie 0.16.8 (numba backend). Each ran at 4 chains x 1,000/1,000 at the sampler defaults, threads/cores 1 and 4, one child process per cell, arms interleaved. Micro: medians of 10^4 individually timed calls with a glue decomposition. Builds: the baseline wheel of `3583d130` and the fix wheel of `06a551a7` (hashes in protocol), in private venvs over the shared harness packages (Python 3.11.16, numpy 2.4.6, numba 0.67.0, JAX 0.10.2, torch 2.13.0+cpu, PyMC 5.28.5, PyTensor 2.38.2), with a private PyTensor compiledir. All walls are contended (about ten agents on 16 cores).
- Seeds: 4747001, 4747002, 4747003 (micro points 4747501), exact-word verified unused in `main` and every `wt/*` branch.
- Status: every cell is recorded and none was rerun. Phase 1: 24 micro + 162 sampler cells, with 6 baseline `thread_safe` threads=4 timeouts (600 s cap, A1), 12 cells not run under A1, and 6 hier `cfunc` errors. Phase 1b: 63 cells, with 12 hier `cfunc`/`cfunc-guarded` errors. Phase 2: 294 cells, with the 6 A1 timeouts again and 12 hier `cfunc-guarded` errors. Phase 1c: 21 cells, all ok.
- Outcome: **merge rule met.** Fix ≡ baseline in 102/102 paired cells. `from_jax` gains 2.05/2.06/2.59x ESS/s at threads=1 (gauss/eight/hier). Python callables at threads=4 gain 1.14–1.90x (torch 1.03–1.09x). `from_pymc(thread_safe=True)` at threads=4 goes from >600 s (one PyTensor compile per callback, because Rayon workers got a fresh `PyThreadState` per `Python::attach`: 2,105 `threading.local` initialisations in 2,105 callbacks) to 1.5–9 s. Untouched cells: 0.95–1.13x.
- Path costs (in-sampler µs per call, gauss/eight/hier, primed): cfunc 1.3/0.6/2.9; `gil_free` 4/6/24; njit callable 3.0/1.8/4.2; numpy 3.8/9.2/36; `from_pymc` 7/12/31; `from_jax` 45/42/82, fixed 22/19/32; `from_torch` 66/292/496. Every Python callable is 1.4–3.6x slower at threads=4 than at threads=1, even after the fix. Compiled paths scale 2.7–3.3x.
- Against nutpie: default `from_pymc` reaches 0.17–0.24x ESS/s at one core; `gil_free` reaches 0.51–1.0x at one core and 0.47–0.93x at four; hand-written cfunc 2.3–16x (different times). nutpie has 1.6–2.4x more ESS per gradient on all three models.
- Usability failures found: a naive numba cfunc port of a working callable dies at transition 0 on hier. Numba's default `error_model="python"` raises `ZeroDivisionError`, which cannot cross the C ABI, and the raw ABI makes a finite value with a nonfinite gradient fatal. `error_model="numpy"` plus a nonfinite guard is bit-identical to the callable path (18/18).
- Predictions: held — P2 (plain Python t4/t1 0.29–0.54, primed 0.14–0.26), P3 at 1/1 (0.17–0.24), P4 (all bit-identity claims), P5 ESS/call agreement, P7, Q1, Q4. Mostly held — P1 13/21, Q2 5/6, Q3 5/9 (gains larger than predicted), Q5 18/19. Failed — P3 at 4/4 (the baseline never finishes), nutpie per-gradient [0.6, 1.6] (actual 1.63–2.35x), P6 (R-hat 1.011–1.014 on two hier seeds, 4 cells).
- Decision/next: merge `06a551a7`; integrations README, CHANGELOG and docstrings rewritten from the measurements. Follow-ups: a guarded `from_cfunc` option; `gil_free=True` as the documented first choice for PyMC; a kernel study of the 1.6–2.4x ESS-per-gradient gap to nutpie. The three `gil_free` freeze tests fail on PyMC 5.28.5 (no `freeze_dims_and_data`), identically on baseline; that is pre-existing. The fix was tested only on Windows/CPython 3.11, so Linux/macOS and Python 3.12+ are left to CI.
