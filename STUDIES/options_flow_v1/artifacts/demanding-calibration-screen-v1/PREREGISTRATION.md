# Demanding fresh-dataset resource screen

This document describes the prepared experiment. Execution requires independently
reviewed frozen plan and manifests plus a pre-execution commit. No sampling result
is implied by preparation.

The question is whether the existing mixed-coordinate continuation procedure is
computationally ready for a larger repeated-dataset calibration study at T1000/G4.
This screen has no calibration qualification or practical-speed decision. Its
datasets are exploratory and cannot be pooled into confirmation after tuning.

The immutable `run-v2/recipe-intent.json` precedes generation of 32 independent
prior datasets. All 32 receive a public fit; indices 0,4,8,12,16,20,24,28 also
receive an associative mixed NumPyro reference fit. Pair members share observations
and four physical starts, with independent sampler/predictive streams. Backend
order alternates across the eight pairs. The 144 streams and exact historical
search scope are retained under `preparation-v2`; no universal seed-exclusion
claim is made beyond that documented inventory.

Keep the existing block32 public recipe and reference target acceptance 0.9,
four chains, 1000 warmup and first-passing retained tiers 1000/2000/4000/8000.
Require all 1012 coordinate diagnostics, 14 physical functionals, four forecasts,
zero retained divergences and unchanged 2% endpoint-MCSE gates. Do not tune on
generated truth, replace failed datasets, rerun failed fits or omit their costs.
Future covariates are synthetic known/scenario inputs, not a real-market claim.

All 40 intended outcomes remain visible. Use contained CPU0 workers with an
1800-second timeout and a 21600-second execution admission budget, reserving
15 seconds for cleanup when admitting each worker. The wrapper owns descendant
cleanup. This is not an independent hard deadline for the entire controller.
Provenance or containment faults stop admission of later workers.

Before every worker, record free bytes and require at least 12884901888 bytes
(12 GiB). A failed space check latches remaining cases unstarted for storage.
This is not a reservation or a full-cohort storage guarantee. Keep partial
outputs and all earlier artifacts. The legacy JSON transport remains in use;
the separate lossless utility does not silently alter this procedure.

Record imports, compilation/parity, adaptation, all attempted retained tiers,
diagnostics, prediction, serialization and wrapper costs. Record normalization,
hashing and reporting separately and add their elapsed cost once to execution.
Unstarted cases, invalid provenance, reporting failure or combined budget overrun
make resource inference unresolved. Ordinary independently contained fit failures
are outcomes. No fixed-size exact binomial claim applies to a cost-dependent
completed prefix.

Report all 18 parameter/forecast selectors, endpoint precision and failures using
the fixed feasibility reporting contract. These small-cohort bounds cannot
establish calibration equivalence, model adequacy, general sampler superiority,
or market usefulness. A later confirmatory study must independently select its
estimands, failure policy, multiplicity allocation, sample size and fresh streams.

Preserve the initial guarded preparation abort. It happened before audit or
fixture generation and is not a sampler failure. `run-v2` is the actual prepared
attempt. Any subsequent amendment must be explicit before affected execution.
