# Saved-data predictive grid feasibility

The bounded probe completed with 20 shared query points in 56.9297 seconds of
worker time, 57.4068 seconds of contained elapsed time and 1,125,511,168 bytes
of peak process committed memory. Both saved reference fits and their exact
4k/8k prefixes used the full 16/32/64/128 quadrature ladder.

All 60,006 declared weight rows resolved their estimated-error inversion brackets
at the prospective .01 absolute-width tolerance. Those rows contained only ten
distinct count vectors and six distinct normalized mixtures. They do not mimic
the weight diversity of a 64-fit bank. Paired prefix counts were unchanged
through refinement. No posterior draws were generated or admitted to a bank.

The maximum observed quadrature differences were about 1.11e-16. These are
convergence estimates at queried points, not certified integration-error bounds.
The result verifies the declared finite scenarios under those estimates; the
flag for all possible weight vectors remains false. The raw-unit .01 width is
a resource-probe choice, not a posterior-accuracy claim or a new bank tolerance.

Config, exact weights, outputs, hardware records and source copies are retained.
The result supports proceeding with the grid implementation and budgeting a
larger analysis phase, but does not prove 64-fit cost, numerical adequacy or
posterior reference coverage. Independent review passed 15 checks and verified
all declared scalar brackets were at most .0078125. The exact auditor, source
ZIP and provenance are archived. Earlier-order CDF arrays were not saved, so
the reported quadrature differences are supported by executed source but cannot
be independently recomputed from this output alone. Future integrated caches
will preserve every order explicitly; this original probe remains unchanged.
