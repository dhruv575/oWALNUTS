### WP22-POSTERIORDB-BENCH-V1 — default-settings posteriordb benchmark: Appendix C warmup rejected as default; DA arm behind CmdStan/nutpie on breadth
- Ordered time: 2026-09-01, preregistration committed (`4020d29`) before the first evidence cell; cells 20:46–22:52 local, strictly sequential; analysis immediately after.
- Protocol/config: `STUDIES/posteriordb_bench_v1/{PREREGISTRATION.md, protocol.json}` (hashes in the study `CHECKSUMS.sha256`). 17 posteriordb posteriors with reference draws (posteriordb `28f8d3d`; `radon_*`/`dogs` have no references and were replaced), 4 chains, 1,000/1,000, every sampler at its defaults: owalnuts-da (BridgeStan `StanTarget`, package-default tuning h0=0.1/depth 8/4 levels/delta 1, DA 0.8, adapted diagonal, 4 threads, uniform(-2,2) starts), owalnuts-paper (same + `PaperAdaptationConfig::default()`), CmdStan 2.39.0 via cmdstanpy 1.3.0 defaults, nutpie 0.16.8 BridgeStan backend defaults. Kernel v10. ArviZ 0.23.4 rank R-hat / bulk / tail ESS over the reference-draw parameter set; gates R-hat <= 1.01, ESS >= 400, zero divergences; agreement z against reference mean with both MCSEs.
- Seeds: 77101, 77102, 77103 for every arm (verified unused before freezing). Deviation 1: the driver process was killed externally during the `lotka_volterra` DA cells and relaunched; the interrupted cell was re-run from scratch (seeded, resumable). Deviation 2 (classification only): `lotka_volterra` paper 77101 is recorded as `timeout` although the harness had written an error result after 20 s and then hung at process exit for the remaining 45 min.
- Status: preregistered negative evidence for the Appendix C default question; preregistered breadth benchmark with external competitors (CmdStan, nutpie); all 204 cells present.
- Outcome: **Appendix C fails the preregistered decision rule** — geomean paper/DA min-bulk-ESS/gradient 0.077 (tail 0.061, ESS/s 0.067) over the 12 models complete on both arms; "at least as good" on 4/12 (two of those are models where both arms fail); paper passes 8/51 cells vs DA 26/51, CmdStan 34/51, nutpie 29/51. On nine models the paper arm froze all or some chains (exactly 128,000 calls, every retained transition a refinement exhaustion). Mechanism from `PaperAdaptationUpdate` telemetry: from uniform(-2,2) starts the first window's orbit energy ranges are 10^3–10^16, the K-quantile rule installs delta ~ 0 at transition 74 and never recovers (no orbit completes, unrefined fraction `None`, h stays 0.1) — the WP17 V3-A freeze mode on ordinary posteriors. Where it did work (noncentered eight schools 3/3, garch11 3/3) it was 0.90–0.95x DA per gradient. Head-to-head: owalnuts-da is 0.32x CmdStan and 0.25x nutpie on bulk ESS/gradient and 0.11x / 0.26x on bulk ESS/s (14 models, 0 wins); the one place oWALNUTS is ahead is `gp_pois_regr`, where both NUTS implementations diverge (4–175 per seed) and both oWALNUTS arms that ran had zero divergences (DA passes 2/3). DA-arm failure modes: step collapse to ~0.003 with 85–90% depth-8 caps on `diamonds`/`earnings` (CmdStan at depth 10 passes with 4x the gradients), `sblrc` R-hat 1.3, `accel_gp` R-hat 1.6; six `arma11` cells and four others died because `StanTarget` treats NaN log density / NaN-inf gradient as fatal where CmdStan and nutpie reject the proposal; two `lotka_volterra` starts were not evaluable (no start retry). Refinement was essentially never engaged (99% of retained leaves at level 0). Predictions: P1 falsified, P2 partially held (fewer but nonzero divergences on centered eight schools), P3 held (by freezing), P4 held. Agreement: every healthy cell of every arm within max |z| 3.3 of the reference.
- Diagnostics: `artifacts/results-table.md` (per-model table, Appendix C ratio table, head-to-head), `artifacts/summary.json` (every cell and metric), `artifacts/cells/*.json` (per-cell metrics incl. per-parameter z, step/delta telemetry, depth and refinement histograms), raw draws hashed and uncommitted.
- Artifacts: `STUDIES/posteriordb_bench_v1/{PREREGISTRATION.md, protocol.json, README.md, LEDGER-ENTRY.md, Cargo.toml, Cargo.lock, src/main.rs, run_posteriordb.py, checksums.py, CHECKSUMS.sha256, artifacts/{summary.json, results-table.md, cells/, measured_on.json, run-log.txt, run-log-2.txt}}`.
- Conclusion: supported — (a) `PaperAdaptationConfig::default()` is not safe as a default warmup: it needs a delta floor and/or a burn-in before the first K-quantile installation, or evaluable-start retries, and must be re-benchmarked after that; (b) at package defaults the oWALNUTS DA arm is behind CmdStan and nutpie on breadth, on gates and by 3–4x per gradient and ~9x per second, with depth 8 + diagonal metric and the BridgeStan-facade per-call overhead as the two identified causes; (c) the divergence-free advantage reappears only on `gp_pois_regr` in this set. Not supported: any breadth-throughput claim for oWALNUTS beyond the eight-schools strict track; any claim that refinement helps on these posteriors (it was not engaged).
- Next decision: (1) do not adopt Appendix C by default; add a delta floor / delayed first installation and start retries, then rerun this exact protocol (same seeds are consumed — use fresh ones); (2) make `StanTarget` map NaN density/gradient to a recoverable rejection and add CmdStan-style start retries in the harness; (3) raise the package default depth to 10 for parity and profile the facade + FFI per-call overhead on `hmm_example`/`arK` where walls are 10–30x CmdStan at equal gradient counts; (4) after (1)–(3), a v2 of this benchmark is the release gate for any breadth claim.
