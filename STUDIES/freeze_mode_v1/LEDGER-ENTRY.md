- Outcome: mechanism -- the seven frozen `arma11` chains start at log density -4.5e19 to -1.8e115 (44 % of uniform(-2,2) starts are past |logp| 4e18 on this model); every downhill leapfrog from there has an astronomically negative energy error, which WALNUTS' two-sided `|H_end - H_start| <= delta` turns into an exhaustion at the initial leaf, and the coarse-endpoint statistic (0) then drives dual averaging to `h ~ 1/|grad|` (1e-20 to 1e-68) where `q + h v == q` and the kinetic energy is below the ulp of `U`: a stable equilibrium at statistic ~0.8 with no position change. Fix -- `ExhaustionRule::AcceptUnlessDivergent` (Stan's test with rises below `|H_0| * 2^-40` counted as zero, Stan's statistic for the kept leaf) applied to warmup only (`WarmupConfig::with_warmup_exhaustion_rule`): `arma11` 0 frozen of 12 on all seeds, min bulk ESS 1,290-1,460 (baseline 4-7), escapes at warmup transitions 6-85; `lotka_volterra` 78101/78103 pass (900/842; baseline 78103 was 6.8), 78102 crawls under every arm. Two-sided arms (`baseline`, `exhaust-accept`, `mean-accept`, `stan-style`, `step-floor`) freeze exactly as v2; the rule combined with Stan's statistic or preset re-freezes chains. Funnel at sampler defaults: retained-phase acceptance of exhausted leaves -- one-sided (z -7.2) or the existing two-sided `AcceptBelowDivergenceThreshold` (z -7.3) -- loses the neck; warmup-only matches the baseline (0.0214 +- 0.014 vs 0.0236 +- 0.014).
