# Mass metric: diagonal regularisation and structured path metrics

## State of knowledge

The diagonal metric the sampler adapts during warmup, and how its variances are regularised, turned out to be the single largest per-gradient lever found in the 0.2 programme. The shipped `v10` regularisation floored every variance at about 0.01, which on regressions with coefficient scales below 0.1 installed a metric up to 1,000x too wide and made dual averaging hold the step 30x below CmdStan's. Stan's regularisation fixes that but cannot ship alone, because at the corrected metric the endpoint U-turn rule ends orbits too early and the warmup window then measures no movement and re-floors the variance. Only the pair `MomentumSum` plus `DiagonalMetricRegularization::Stan` works, and it is now the default. On state-space targets a different lever applies: a posterior-precision tridiagonal path block is the correct conditional metric and is confirmed at 2.2 to 2.7x per call, but refreshing it per window and the wider arrowhead and pooled lines are either falsified or frozen. Details and every number below are in the [ledger](../research-ledger-2026-08-31.md).

## Timeline of evidence

- **WP4** `STUDIES/exact_state_space_ground_truth_v1`: on a centred Gaussian local-level path with an exact tridiagonal posterior, arms identity, posterior-variance diagonal, posterior-precision bidiagonal Cholesky (P) and prior-precision only (Q). Cap and no-cap predictions held 16/16. P mixes at depth 3 to 4 with ESS per call 4.8x the identity arm and about 1,000x Q; Q caps 92 to 93 percent at T=1000 with a step of 0.0026. The T=1000 phenomenology in the older Polyscope ledger is reproduced by prior-based path metrics and eliminated by the posterior-precision block.
- **WP4b** `STUDIES/real_target_path_metric_v1`: on the real canonical-v2 target, arm P gives 2.8x ESS per call over the adapted diagonal on sspd-11 with the globals free, and 50 to 200x with the globals frozen at their posterior means. A more accurate path block with the globals free (P2) was worse than P everywhere, which located the residual problem in global-to-path scale coupling rather than the path block.
- **WP12** `STUDIES/sspd11_confirmation_v1`: fresh-seed confirmation. Arm I (adapted diagonal) passed 3/3. Arm P passed 2/3 with the single miss a rank R-hat of 1.0102 on one functional; efficiency P over I 2.57, 2.96, 2.68, geometric mean 2.735. Not confirmed under the conjunctive rule.
- **WP16** `STUDIES/sspd11_refreshed_block_v1`: the boundary-refresh driver `sample_chains_structured_refresh` is exact (identity refresh bit-identical to the fixed driver). Arm P confirmed 3/3 at 4x500/4,000 at 2.2 to 2.3x arm I, closing WP12's open confirmation as a run-length effect. Per-window refresh (arm R) not confirmed, 2/3, with ESS per call only 0.25 to 0.52x P; the failure is estimation, since early slow windows at small steps underestimate a slow global's variance and install precision entries up to about 2,000.
- **WP19** and **WP21** `STUDIES/flagship_crypto_sv_v1`, `STUDIES/flagship_crypto_sv_v2`: a one-shot tridiagonalised-global plus AR(1)-and-curvature path precision metric on five crypto stochastic-volatility series. Fastest wall on 5/5 assets, but the global (a, s) ridge with correlation about -0.9 bottlenecks every backend, and nutpie and NumPyro's windowed adaptation extract about 1.5 to 2x more global ESS at T of about 3,150. WP21 selected arm E (paper adaptation) from a pilot factorial; arm D arrowhead pilots were the best per-call mechanism but stayed at the pilot tier.
- **WP27** `STUDIES/step_collapse_v1`: diagnosis of the `sblrc` and `earnings` step collapse. The `v10` regularisation `(n/(n+5)) var + 5/(n+5)` floors every variance at 0.0099 for n = 500; `sblrc`'s coefficients have posterior variance 1.07e-5, so the installed metric is 1,000x too wide and dual averaging correctly holds h at 0.003 against CmdStan's 0.10. Every step-side remedy left h at 0.003 to 0.007; `DiagonalMetricRegularization::Stan` alone gave h 0.10 to 0.12 and 9x ESS per gradient on `sblrc`. On `earnings` it reached CmdStan's step but failed R-hat at a quarter of CmdStan's per-draw ESS because the WALNUTS orbit was 49 leaves against NUTS's 163 at that metric. Kept opt-in.
- **WP29** `STUDIES/posteriordb_bench_v4`: Stan regularisation alone on the full 17-model set. `sblrc` 7.76x and `arma11` 2.48x, geomean 1.158x the default, but `earnings` 0.35x at 0/3 gates, `diamonds` 0.71x, and five models lost gates (`earnings`, `kidiq`, `diamonds`, `hmm_drive_0`, `one_comp`): 33 versus 39 cells. Not a default on its own.
- **WP30** `STUDIES/kernel_gap_v1`: at CmdStan's own step, metric and starts, the kernel-side gap is leaves per orbit under the endpoint U-turn statistic; `MomentumSum` recovers 0.77x to 0.90x. This is the other half of the metric finding.
- **WP31** `STUDIES/joint_default_v1`: the pair `MomentumSum` plus `Stan` regularisation. C1 held at 1.508x the default per gradient (rhosum alone 1.116, stanreg alone 1.257), 41 versus 35 gates, `earnings` 3.69x and 3/3, `sblrc` 9.12x, funnel and Eight Schools safe. The preregistered "no model below 0.85x" clause failed on `hmm_drive_0` (a mode lottery) and the centred eight schools (fails every arm). Mechanism of the interaction: `stanreg` alone on `earnings` installs mass entries of 9.5e4 to 9.8e4 (the Stan floor) because short endpoint-rule orbits leave the window variance unmeasured; under `MomentumSum` the window measures it and installs 5.8e3 to 6.2e3, which is CmdStan's 1.6e-4 variance.
- **WP32** `STUDIES/posteriordb_bench_v5`: the post-hoc default change validated on fresh seeds against rerun CmdStan and nutpie. 42/51 gates versus 36 and 28, `sblrc` 0/3 to 3/3 at h 0.10 to 0.12, 2.01x the old default per gradient, no model below 0.8x, funnel exact. The honest per-gradient figure against CmdStan on healthy models is 0.82x.

## What worked

| change | study | result | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DiagonalMetricRegularization::Stan` together with `UTurnRule::MomentumSum` as the `sampler` default | WP31, WP32 | 1.508x per gradient over 17 models, 42/51 gates, `sblrc` 9x, `earnings` 3/3 | Stan's regularisation removes the absolute variance floor; the momentum-sum U-turn keeps orbits long enough at the corrected metric for each window to measure the variance instead of re-flooring it |
| Posterior-precision tridiagonal path block (`BidiagonalCholesky`) on state-space targets | WP4, WP4b, WP12, WP16 | exact Gaussian: 4.8x identity, no caps; real sspd-11: 2.2 to 2.8x, confirmed 3/3 at 4x500/4,000 | it is the conditional posterior metric of the path given the globals, so the condition number that forces about T leapfrogs per U-turn disappears |
| Boundary-refresh driver `sample_chains_structured_refresh` | WP16 | identity refresh bit-identical to the fixed driver; 16/16 installs, zero refresh failures | exact installation seam, kept as an opt-in mechanism even though the refresh policy failed |
| Cached initial evaluation and per-transition workspaces (not a metric change, but the same lever's overhead side) | programme 2026-09-04 | draws bit-identical, allocations per call 8.5 to 14.4 down to 0.19 to 1.27 | engineering, no default semantics changed |

## What did not work

| change | study | result | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The `v10` unit-variance floor `(n/(n+5)) var + 5/(n+5)` | WP27 | `sblrc` h 0.003 versus CmdStan 0.10; `earnings` three of five variances floored | an absolute floor at 0.0099 is 1,000x the true variance on small-scale regression coefficients |
| Stan regularisation alone | WP27, WP29 | `earnings` 0.35x and 0/3, `kidiq` 3 to 0 gates, five models lose gates | at the corrected metric the endpoint U-turn orbit is too short (49 leaves versus 163), the window sees no movement, and the metric overshoots by 1e2 on one chain per seed |
| Step-side remedies for the collapse (Stan's statistic, `init_stepsize` per window, delta ramp, `mu = ln 10h`, floors, shrink bounds) | WP27 | h stays at 0.003 to 0.007 on `sblrc` | the step was correct for the installed metric; the metric was wrong |
| Per-window refresh of the path block (arm R) | WP16 | 2/3, ESS per call 0.25 to 0.52x the one-shot block | early slow windows at small steps underestimate a slow global's variance and install precision entries up to about 2,000; boundary step searches oscillate |
| Prior-precision-only path metric (the older local AR(1) analogue) | WP4 | 92 to 93 percent depth caps at T=1000, step 0.0026 | the level direction is the stiff one; the prior alone misses the observation term |
| A more accurate path block with globals free (P2) | WP4b | worse than P everywhere, 66 percent caps on sspd-10 | innovations are in absolute units in every centredness, so all parameterisations share the `sigma_x` to 0 funnel; the residual is global-to-path coupling |
| One-shot structured metric on the crypto SV globals | WP19, WP21 | about 1.5 to 2x less global ESS than nutpie and NumPyro at T of about 3,150 | the (a, s) ridge needs windowed re-estimation that a one-shot calibration does not provide |
| Mutable and pooled arrowhead line | programme 2026-08-31, WP21 arm D | frozen at stage 7; arm D pilots best per call but never left the pilot tier | the adaptation confound had to be removed first; a preregistered confirmation was never run |

## Current default

`sampler::Tuning::default()` uses `UTurnRule::MomentumSum`, and `Adaptation::default()` uses `DiagonalMetricRegularization::Stan`, adopted post hoc after WP31 and validated by WP32. The `walnutpie` facade defaults and every kernel fingerprint are unchanged. The structured path block, the refresh driver and the arrowhead facades remain opt-in, the last behind the `research` feature. Stan's regularisation should not be offered alone.

## Open questions

- A scale-free regularisation toward the window's median variance was named in WP27 as an alternative default candidate and has not been measured.
- `diamonds` at depth 10 is capped in every arm at h about 0.003 to 0.005 and is not a floor case; the depth cap itself is the item there (WP31, WP32).
- The global-to-path scale coupling on state-space targets is the mechanism behind the arrowhead line; it needs a generalisation beyond a handful of globals, Python exposure and a preregistered confirmation (WP21).
- A final-boundary-only rebuild of the path block from the longest window, or arm P with a calibrated globals diagonal, is the candidate production path named in WP16 and is unmeasured.
