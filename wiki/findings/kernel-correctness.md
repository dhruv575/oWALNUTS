# Kernel correctness: oracles, the funnel bias, and error semantics

## State of knowledge

The kernel is held correct by differential oracles against the upstream `walnutpie` implementation (commit `f5bba365`) and by bit-identity fingerprints, not by argument. Two real defects were found this way and fixed: the `v8` micro-step acceptance statistic was not time-reversal symmetric and over-weighted Neal's funnel neck by about a factor of two (fixed in `v9`), and through `v9` a recoverable target failure stopped the whole transition instead of refining through it as upstream does (fixed in `v10`). Since `v10` the frozen `walnutpie` defaults and `ALGORITHM_REVISION` have not changed; every performance and driver change during the 0.2 programme had to reproduce the pinned fingerprints bit for bit. The remaining correctness questions are policy questions at the boundary of the kernel: which exhaustion rule applies in which phase, and what to do with a non-finite proposed position.

## Timeline of evidence

- **WP2-FUNNEL-REPRO-V1** (`STUDIES/paper_funnel_reproduction_v1`): at the paper's fixed tuning (h = 0.36, delta = 0.21, 10 levels, depth 10) oWALNUTS placed P(omega < −5) = 0.0959 against the exact 0.0478 and 0.0971 at 4 x 50,000 (z about 10.7); the Flatiron reference at identical tuning was exact (0.0477, z −0.01). The no-refinement control at h = 0.11 reproduced the paper's NUTS failure (0.0163, omega never below −5.7). The bias was absent on a Gaussian and absent with refinement disabled, so it was localised to the refinement/reverse-check path.
- **WP6-FUNNEL-BIAS-FIX-V9** (`STUDIES/funnel_bias_fix_v1`): root cause found by a 4,000-leaf funnel oracle generated from the upstream headers (`oracle/walnutpie/f5bba365_funnel_leaves`). `kernel::integrate` accepted a micro-step on max_j |H(z_j) − H(z_0)| over every visited micro-step; upstream `macro_step`/`within_tolerance` use |H(end) − H(start)| only. The path-wide statistic is not symmetric under time reversal, so the reverse selection could differ from the forward one while the kernel only re-checked coarser levels, and non-reversible leaves were accepted. `v8` disagreed with the oracle on 1,555 of 4,000 leaves; `v9` agrees on all 4,000 to 1e-11. F50 after the fix: P(omega < −5) 0.0474 (z −0.08), P(omega < −6) 0.0223 (z −0.14), var(omega) 9.04, 8.2M calls against 12.8M under `v8`. No locked fingerprint changed because the frozen default tuning never reaches a leaf where the two statistics differ.
- **WP8-EIGHT-SCHOOLS-V9-REBENCH-V1** (`STUDIES/eight_schools_v9_rebench_v1`): paired re-measurement on the v38 strict track. ESS per target call `v9`/`v7` 0.961 bulk, 0.986 tail, total calls within 1 % per seed: the correction is cost-neutral. The published 19,054.65 / 14,494.34 ESS/s figures were medians mislabelled as conservative minima; the true `v7` minima were 8,634 / 5,949 and the `v9` minimum is 12,830 / 10,346, still 2.04x CmdStan bulk. An erratum was drafted in `RELEASE-NOTE.md`.
- **WP2b-SW-REPRO-V1** (`STUDIES/paper_stock_watson_reproduction_v1`): on a simulated Stock–Watson series the paper's fixed tuning refined 4–5 halvings below h/8 on 86 % of transitions with 44 % reverse-coarser rejections, and 13.2 % of orbits had an energy range above 2. Three defects were recorded: recoverable evaluations stopped the transition instead of failing the micro tolerance; the Appendix C h rule counted zero-leaf transitions as unrefined and drove h to its 1e6 bound; the per-transition Hamiltonian range for exhausted transitions was constant per chain.
- **WP10-INVALID-EVALUATION-PARITY-V10** (`STUDIES/invalid_evaluation_parity_v1`): through `v9` a `TargetError::recoverable` became a NaN evaluation and a `StopReason::InvalidEvaluation`, counted as a divergence. Upstream maps a failed evaluation to logp = −inf, grad = 0, so the endpoint error is infinite, the leaf refines, and only `build_leaf` failing after every halving stops the orbit in that direction. `v10` implements this: −inf with a finite gradient is a zero-density point that integration continues through, excluded from the Hamiltonian extrema and divergence statistic. A second 4,000-leaf oracle (`oracle/walnutpie/f5bba365_invalid_leaves`, 343 wall-touching leaves) agrees to 1e-11. Truncated 2-D Gaussian: 5,061,666 recoverable evaluations, 0 invalid stops, 0 divergences, every draw inside, moments within sampling variation. Stock–Watson F: 3,404,887 recoverable evaluations (16.5 % of retained calls) with 0 invalid stops.
- **WP14-NUMPYRO-COMPARISONS-V10-V1** (`STUDIES/numpyro_comparisons_v10_v1`): NumPyro NUTS under-covers the funnel neck on 6/6 cells (P(omega < −5) = 0.0000 in five, 0.0115 in one; 365–3,449 divergences per cell); oWALNUTS at fixed paper tuning is exact 3/3 (0.0461–0.0510, 0 divergences).
- **WP24-FREEZE-MODE-V1** (`STUDIES/freeze_mode_v1`): the `arma11` freeze is the two-sided leaf test |H_end − H_start| <= delta turning an astronomically negative downhill energy error into an exhaustion at the initial leaf. Stan's one-sided rule (`ExhaustionRule::AcceptUnlessDivergent`) applied to warmup only removes it. Applied to retained draws it loses the funnel neck (z −7.2; the two-sided `AcceptBelowDivergenceThreshold` z −7.3), so the retained rule stays `Stop`.
- **WP28-FUNNEL-DEFAULTS-V1** (`STUDIES/funnel_defaults_v1`): the 0.2.0 sampler defaults with four refinement levels were biased low on the funnel on two of three seeds (z −3.5, −3.75; 2,933 retained exhaustions). Eight levels are within |z| <= 2 on every seed at 1.05x Eight Schools and 1.00x Gaussian cost, and became the default. WP29 later confirmed levels 5–8 are selected by 0.000 of retained transitions on 15 of 16 posteriordb models.
- **WP30-KERNEL-GAP-V1** and **WP33-CHAIN-RESCUE-V1**: the initial-evaluation cache and the `ChainRun` driver refactor were accepted because draws were bit-identical to the previous driver; optimisations that would have changed floating-point operation order were rejected.
- **SSPD-TARGET-FATAL-DIAG-V1** (`STUDIES/sspd_target_fatal_diag_v1`): the WP37B stop at the state-space target was the frozen target's fatal classification of reachable non-finite results (12/13 diagnostic seeds fatal at step 0.5), plus a second gap: the kernel aborts the run with `ErrorKind::Numerical` on a non-finite proposed position, which Stan would treat as a divergent leaf.
- **WP38-NONFINITE-POSITION-POLICY-V1** (`STUDIES/nonfinite_position_policy_v1`): research-only `NonfinitePositionPolicy::RejectLeaf` completes all 24 runs on the repaired state-space target where `Abort` fails 2/24, with one or two warmup-only rejected leaves, and is bit-identical to `Abort` on every cell without the event (22/22). The registered health gate failed 0/24 because it measured the target's own mixing (identical draws on both arms), so the policy is not qualified and `Abort` stays the default.

## What worked

| change | study | result | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Endpoint-only micro-step acceptance (`v9`) | WP6 `funnel_bias_fix_v1` | 4,000/4,000 oracle agreement; funnel tail z −0.08 at 4 x 50,000; 36 % fewer calls | forward and reverse selections use the same endpoint statistic, restoring reversibility |
| Zero-density refine-through for recoverable failures (`v10`) | WP10 `invalid_evaluation_parity_v1` | 5.06M recoverable evaluations, 0 invalid stops, exact truncated moments | matches upstream `NoExceptLogpGrad` semantics; leapfrog with zero force on the region stays a reversible involution |
| Eight refinement levels as default | WP28 `funnel_defaults_v1`, WP29 | funnel unbiased on every seed; free on posteriordb (levels 5–8 never selected) | four halvings of the adapted step cannot enter the neck |
| One-sided exhaustion rule in warmup only | WP24 `freeze_mode_v1`, WP25 | zero frozen chains on 51 cells; `arma11` 0/3 to 2/3, `lotka_volterra` 1/3 to 3/3 | Stan's transition-relative test lets a chain ride downhill from a bad start |
| Bit-identity as the acceptance rail for refactors | WP30, WP33 | cache and driver refactor accepted; reorderings rejected | `tests/kernel_fingerprint.rs` pins the frozen defaults |
| Differential leaf oracles against `f5bba365` | WP6, WP10 | two kernel defects localised to the leaf level and fixed | disagreement is attributable per leaf, not per posterior |

## What did not work

| change | study | result | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Path-wide max energy error as the leaf tolerance (`v8`) | WP2, WP6 | twice the funnel neck mass | not symmetric under time reversal; non-reversible leaves accepted |
| Mapping recoverable failures to a transition stop (`v9` and earlier) | WP2b, WP10 | no-op transitions on stiff models; Stock–Watson needed target-side emulation | upstream refines through −inf; stopping counts it as a divergence |
| One-sided exhaustion rule on retained draws | WP24 | funnel neck lost (z −7.2) | accepting an exhausted leaf in the retained phase biases the tail |
| Lower `delta` (0.5, 0.25) at four levels as a funnel fix | WP28 | bias worse, 11,000–12,000 exhaustions | tighter tolerance without more halvings only exhausts more |
| `RejectLeaf` on a non-finite proposed position as a qualified opt-in | WP38 | completes every run but health gate fails 0/24 | the gate measured the target's mixing, not the policy |
| Publishing across-seed medians as conservative minima | WP8 | erratum | aggregation rule differed from the competitors' true minima |

## Current state

The kernel is at revision `walnutpie-warmup-telemetry-tau0.6-m1-r2-e1-d3-v10` and has not changed since 0.1.0-beta.2; every pinned fingerprint and oracle still holds. The funnel marginal is reproduced at the paper's tuning and at the sampler defaults with eight levels. Recoverable target failures refine through as upstream does; NaN, +inf and non-finite gradients remain fatal. `ExhaustionRule::AcceptUnlessDivergent` applies to warmup transitions only; the retained rule is `Stop`. `NonfinitePositionPolicy::Abort` is the default and `RejectLeaf` is research-only. The `StopReason::InvalidEvaluation` variant is unreachable for successful runs and may be removed at the next breaking release.

## Open questions

- Whether a non-finite proposed position should become a divergent leaf by default, decided by a fresh preregistration with a paired health gate (each `reject`-only cell within the completed-pair distribution).
- The per-chain step collapse on the funnel at the sampler defaults (one chain per seed at h about 0.001–0.01, omega R-hat 1.01–1.04): unbiased pooled, not efficient. WP28, WP31 and WP32 all carry the caveat.
- Whether any refinement-active result from before `v9` (Neal funnel pilots v1–v3) should be re-run before being cited; WP6 marked them provisional.
- The frozen `sspd11_confirmation_v1` target copy classifies reachable non-finite results as fatal; any renewed state-space protocol must repair or omit it.
